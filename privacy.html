Privacy Policy for FastForm

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Summary: FastForm is designed with a 100% Privacy-First approach. All user data, profiles, and form templates are stored strictly on your local browser device. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data.

1. Information Collection and Use

FastForm does not collect, log, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information created within the extension (such as names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, custom keywords) is saved strictly within Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your machine.

2. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used solely to save your autofill profile templates locally on your browser.
  • activeTab & scripting: Used only when you press Alt+A or click 'Fill Form Now' to detect form input signatures on the active page and populate your saved profile values.
  • <all_urls>: Allows FastForm to function universally on any website where form autofilling is requested by the user.

3. Third-Party Services and Ads

FastForm contains a non-intrusive sponsored advertisement link area. Clicking sponsored links redirects you to third-party partner websites. We do not share any user profile data or browsing history with advertising partners.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third party under any circumstances.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@fastform-ext.com
